Lower cap → large pinion bottom

The upper cap meets the small pinion top; the lower cap meets the large pinion bottom. Both sections share one shaft outside the motor bodies. Its working end extends outward beyond the small pinion. In motor mode, the counter-rotating rings drive this output shaft.

See the shaft. Shape the idea.

Use 3D orientation to inspect two frameless motor rings, their geared caps, and the shared shaft from different angles. Use 2D sketch to arrange parts and draw freely. Both views share the same design.

Field view cuts a section through the shared magnet band of the design plan's Addendum 2 and computes its magnetic field exactly for ideal uniformly magnetized blocks in air — no iron, no saturation, no eddy currents. Turn the band, tilt the bars, change the remanence, grade the band's magnetization toward one machine with the bias slider, or click a magnet in the section to flip it, and watch the field lines and the two airgap readings follow. The stators are drawn, not modelled; in air they change nothing.

Motor or generator

Motor drive: electrical input → rings → output shaft. Generator: mechanical input → shaft → rings → electrical output.

The mode changes the intended power flow, not the chosen spin directions. Generator mode is conceptual; there is no electrical simulation. Driven permanent-magnet machines require suitable power electronics to deliver usable electrical output.

Build the motor concept

  • Motor + gear cap adds a frameless motor ring with an oversized flat cap. The cap edge turns downward into a toothed skirt.
  • The higher, larger cap meets the top of the small pinion section. The lower, smaller cap meets the bottom of the large section. These are intended contact locations in the schematic. Both sections share one shared shaft outside both motor bodies.
  • Point shaft toward rings changes the shaft heading at its current position. Its two pinion sections stay together on one shaft.
  • Select a part to edit its size, teeth, color, and orientation in Selection. For a compound pinion, Shaft extension adds length beyond the base shaft, and Working end chooses the small-gear or large-gear end. The current layouts extend the small-gear end outward by 120 drawing units without moving either gear section.
  • Camera buttons and Orbit, Tilt, and Roll sliders change where you look from. They do not change a part's orientation.
  • Drag the 3D view to orbit. Shift-drag to pan. Scroll to zoom, or use the zoom buttons and Fit.

Sketch and share

  • In 2D sketch, drag a part to move it. Use its handles to rotate or resize it.
  • Draw and Erase work in 2D. Choose your ink color and brush size. In 3D, your drawing appears on the flat XY sketch plane.
  • Preview rotation animates the chosen directions. Motion is illustrative and does not solve gear meshing.
  • Save keeps an editable JSON file. Load opens it again. Export PNG or SVG to share a drawing.
